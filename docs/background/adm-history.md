# A Short History of ADM


ADM was initiated a (around) 201x by developers at BBC R&D as it became clear that audio will not just go beyond conventional 2.0 and 5.1 representations, but also demand regarding speech intelligibility and accessibility will require more flexible and intelligent approaches from production to delivery.

The idea of addressing these challenges with "object-based" approach, i.e. by accompagnying metadata that can control a renderer while offering the listeners interactivity for personalisation, provided an ideal concept.

Here some milestones of the developmemt

- 2015: [ITU-R BS. 2076-0](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.2076-0-201506-S/en) was first published in 6 languages. This recommendation by the ITU Radiocommunications Assemby marks the birth of ADM in the public. It corresponds to [ITU-R BS.2015: Advanced sound system for programme production](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.2051-0-201402-S/en) that, for the first time, outlined the scope of future audio systems for digital broadcasting and digital cinema.
