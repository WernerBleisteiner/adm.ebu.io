# ADM PLUGFEST No 2  

The second ADM Plugfest is to take place between Nov 5 and Dec 5 2026, kicking-off during the *MXF Plugfest* at HR-ARD Frankfurt (where some of our members participate)



## CONCEPTION

### Interoperability

The primary objective of this plugfest is to exercise the ability of various commercial and non-commercial tools to handle the:

#### 1. ITU-R BS.2168-0 Advanced Sound Systems Emission Profile.
The ITU-R BS.2168-0 standard defines various operating levels.

#### 2. Tech 3393 EBU Production Profile
Initial interoperability testing for EBU Tech 3393 Production Profile 


#### 3. ARD/ZDF ADM4Legacy Profile (see here and sort latest for draft of the profile doc)
Initial interoperability testing for exemplary configurations defined in the  (Draft) "ADM4Legacy Profile A".
