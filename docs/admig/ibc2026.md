## ADM-IG@IBC2026

*ADM-IG* hit the floor at *IBC 2026*  with quite a number of members showing how ADM/S-ADM is alreday implemented in their pioneering products.

![](img/ADM-IG@EBU@IBC-2026v09.png)


EBU generously hosted us during IBC 2026 at their booth and gave us opportunity to present ADM/S-ADM workflows and applications.

We were also invited to pitch ADM within 5 minutes at EBU's traditional *Open Source Meetup*.
You may catch-up on that at [EBU's web page](https://tech.ebu.ch/events/2026/open-source-meetup-ebu-ibc-2026)



### DEMOS @ EBU 10.D21
Experts from ADM-IG partners came-by to demo ADM applications and workflows at our stand, too. First on Friday morning was Aimee Moulson from Fraunhofer IIS.
ADM-IG Co-Chair Daniele Airola from RAI and Satoshi Oode from NHK welcomed her.
